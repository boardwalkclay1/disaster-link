What is SafeLink?

SafeLink is a simple emergency‑coordination platform designed to help people communicate, request help, organize supplies, and stay informed during natural disasters or community emergencies.

How SafeLink Helps You

Mark Yourself Safe

Quickly mark your location and status so friends, family, and community leaders know you’re okay — or if you need help.

Request Help

If you lose power, run out of food or water, or need medical supplies, SafeLink lets you describe your situation and get assistance fast.

Find Supply Locations

See nearby distribution points for food, water, medicine, blankets, and emergency supplies.

Donate to People in Need

Support individuals or families directly, or contribute to large community campaigns during major disasters.

Community Leaders

Verified leaders can broadcast updates, organize supply drops, and coordinate volunteers.

Emergency Alerts

Receive real‑time updates about danger zones, evacuation routes, shelter openings, and supply deliveries.

Get Started

Create an account or log in to access your dashboard.
